Back when I was growing up (albeit late by the standards of the true old timers), stuff like guestbooks, shoutout boxes and forums, was cool.

Although I truly doubt we'd ever be able to recapture those times, I'm glad I had the pleasure of seeing them and living through them; the times when the internet wasn't this overreaching entity but rather a place to be explored; a place where we didn't hide behind personas but rather used them as an avatar of who we were behind the keyboard; a place where meeting someone from the other side of the world wasn't a daily occurrence and was something we eagerly cherished.

It was either that or I'm truly having an old man yells at the clouds moment. Either way, you can leave a message, if you'd like - the form is at the bottom of this page. :)
