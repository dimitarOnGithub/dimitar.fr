502 - Bad Gateway

So, let me put it this way: the website isn't exactly dead, but it might as well have been.

Backend is nowhere to be found and this poor webserver could very well be illustrated like that scene from Titanic where the band keeps on playing as the ship sinks.

Truth is, I don't know for how long you'd be able to even get to this page; maybe these are its final words, its swan song, the epilogue...

All you have left to do after this moment is hope - hope I was smart enough to have some sort of failover strategy or monitoring. Only time will tell.
